\gold\boldPROJECT ORGANIZATION\reset

\aqua\bold1. Discussion\reset
Ideas are usually born in voice chat or in the members chat. If they generate interest, a forum thread is opened on Discord, under \dark_gray#\boldprojects\reset.

\aqua\bold2. Planning\reset
\yellow• Redstone and farms: lag, mechanics and design validity are evaluated.
\yellow• Decoration and building: spawn proofing, survival viability and terrain integration are considered.

\aqua\bold3. Testing in Mirror\reset
The design is tested in MMP before reaching survival.

\aqua\bold4. Execution\reset
Implementation depends on priority and on not accumulating too many unfinished projects. When it is time to execute, admins publish clear instructions in \dark_gray#\boldmembers-info\reset.

\gold\boldHOW TO KNOW WHAT TO DO?\reset

You are free to work on what you know how to do, as long as it is on an active server task. If you are new, help with main projects first to get familiar with the dynamics.

\yellow\boldCheck active tasks:\reset
\yellow• Review the task threads in \dark_gray#\boldmembers-info\reset\yellow.
\grayIf there are no active threads, there are no pending tasks.
