\gold\boldTRANSPORT\reset

Long-distance transport in Aeternum is done through the \boldNether\reset. The base infrastructure is the \boldPiston Bolt\reset; the \boldPearl Cannon\reset is also used.

\aqua\boldPISTON BOLT\reset
There is one station for each major project. The Nether Hub is the main station. Each station has a map, signs, and a lectern with a numbered book.

\yellow\bold1. Basic use — Switching branches\reset
1. Look at the map and choose a station.
2. Point the arrow at the sign with the correct name and direction.
3. Choose the station number in the book.
4. Get in the minecart.

\yellow\bold2. Advanced use — Same branch\reset
\greenGreen\reset = stations with a higher number.
\redRed\reset = stations with a lower number.
Press the note block to toggle the color.
Select the station difference in the book.
The Nether Hub is always station 0.

\aqua\boldPEARL CANNON\reset
It is in front of the old Main Storage station. Select the destination with note blocks, stand against the trapdoor and the glass pane, look straight up, throw your pearl and do not move.

\red\boldWarnings\reset
\red• Do not use Shift when throwing.
\red• Do not move after throwing.
\red• Do not change destinations if there are players in the area.

\grayIn multiple mode, activate the left lever, everyone positions themselves and throws their pearl; lower the lever when all have been thrown.
