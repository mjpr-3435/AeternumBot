\aqua\boldAeternum Carpet\reset
\yellow/cs\reset
Toggles temporary camera/spectator mode (requires \yellowcameraMode\reset enabled).
\yellow/carefulbreak <off|sneak|always>\reset
Sends mined drops directly into your inventory (off, while sneaking, or always).
\yellow/hat\reset
Equips the item held in your hand onto your head.
\yellow/nether\reset, \yellow/overworld\reset, \yellow/end\reset
Teleports between dimensions preserving relative coordinates (Creative or Spectator only).
\yellow/quickshulker\reset
Toggles opening shulkers directly from your hand when used in the air. Only changes your preference; does not affect other players.
\yellow/quickenderchest\reset
Toggles opening the Ender chest directly from your hand when used in the air. Only changes your preference.
\yellow/find <place> <item>\reset
Searches for the item in the indicated storage area (with place autocomplete) or use \yellownear_me\reset to search within a 20-block radius around you. Shows the containers where it appears, their coordinates and a temporary path with particles.
\yellow/count <place> <item>\reset
Counts how many items and stacks are in the indicated storage area or in \yellownear_me\reset, including content inside shulkers. Does not show a path or modify inventories.
\dark_graySee the \whiteFinder\dark_gray guide to understand searches, paths, inventory updates and storage areas.
\yellow/guide\reset
Opens the server guide library. See \whiteGuides\reset to learn how it is organized and where to find each topic.
\yellow/actions\reset
Opens the available action packages. You can review them and run one with ▶.
\yellow!!<full package name>\reset
Directly runs an Actions package from chat; the name is case-insensitive. See the \whiteActions\reset guide before using important packages.
\yellow/materials\reset
Opens the project material lists. See the \whiteMaterials\dark_gray guide to understand lists, claims, storage areas and the HUD overlay.
\yellow/projects\reset
Opens the server project manager and its linked \yellow.litematic\reset files.
\yellow/stats scoreboard <category> <stat>\reset
Displays a quick Minecraft stat on the sidebar (e.g. \yellowmined stone\reset). Use \yellow/stats scoreboard hide\reset to hide it.
\yellow/waypoints [list|add|del|tp]\reset
Manages global cross-dimension waypoints with quick teleportation.
\yellow/carefulbreak <off|sneak|always>\reset
Sends mined drops directly into your inventory (off, while sneaking, or always).
\yellow/hat\reset
Equips the item held in your hand onto your head.
\yellow/nether\reset, \yellow/overworld\reset, \yellow/end\reset
Teleports between dimensions preserving relative coordinates (Creative or Spectator only).
\dark_grayTip: press Left Alt to open Aeternum Dock for quick access to server screens (when quickDock is enabled).

\aqua\boldBackups\reset

\aquaRegional backups\reset
